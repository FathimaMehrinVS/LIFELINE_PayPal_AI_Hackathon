# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

Record only what works on the day. Check [integration-status.md](integration-status.md) first, and remove any line
that has not been shown working. Use an invented situation.

| Time | Screen | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home | The headline | "Small NGOs receive emergency requests as messy messages. LIFELINE turns one into a plan an NGO can approve and a donor can fund." |
| 0:20 | Request Assistance | Speak or type the situation, then tap the answers | "Someone in a crisis shouldn't have to fill in a long form. AI pulls out the facts and asks only what blocks a decision." |
| 0:50 | AI review, submit | Needs, urgency, unknowns | "Anything not stated stays unknown. AI never guesses a fact." |
| 1:05 | Assistance Cases | The case in the grid, then its detail | "Rules compare delivery routes and cap the basket. The AI only explains." |
| 1:30 | Case detail | Approve | "A person decides. AI can't approve anything or move money." |
| 1:45 | Approved Plans | The new plan and its detail | "Donors see the need, items, cost and progress. Never a name, address, photo or the family's words." |
| 2:05 | PayPal Sandbox | Approve the payment (only if capture works) | "Our server confirms with PayPal before recording anything." |
| 2:25 | Payments, My Contributions | Money in and money out, the timeline | "A donation is never shown as a supplier payment or a delivery. Unrecorded steps stay unrecorded." |
| 2:45 | Audit Logs | Recent events | "Every approval and change is recorded. Simulated steps are labelled." |
| 2:55 | Close | Home | "AI understands, rules verify, NGOs decide, donors fund." |
