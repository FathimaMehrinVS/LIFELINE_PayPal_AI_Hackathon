# Project overview

## Problem

People in crisis often know what they need but not how to get money and essentials to where they are.
Routes differ in fees, speed and accessibility, and shopping on a tight budget is hard.

## What LIFELINE does

1. **Understand** — the Situation Agent reads a plain-language message and extracts needs, budget,
   urgency and anything missing.
2. **Route** — Financial Intelligence compares payment routes on fees, accessibility and risk.
3. **Shop** — Commerce Intelligence builds a basket of essentials that fits the budget.
4. **Pay** — the payment is created and captured through PayPal Sandbox.

## Principles

- AI extracts, compares, ranks and explains. It never authorizes a payment on its own.
- Fee calculations, risk rules and payment approval are deterministic code.
- Every module validates its input and returns errors in one shared format.
